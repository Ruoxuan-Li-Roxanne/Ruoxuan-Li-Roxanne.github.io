👓 **Meta**, Data Scientist, Product Analytics Intern, May 2025 - Aug 2025

💻 **IQVIA**, Analytics and AI Solutions Intern, May 2024 - Aug 2024

- Engineered 200+ features and selected 51 based on dimension reduction like PCA and NMF, correlation analysis, and predefined rules to enhance model performance.
